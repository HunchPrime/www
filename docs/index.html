About Hunchprime

Hunchprime is a small, independent software studio. We build native iOS and iPadOS applications, with a particular focus on games and tools that quietly improve everyday moments.

Our approach is deliberate: we ship fewer apps, but we maintain them carefully. Every release goes through manual review on physical devices, and every product is built to feel native to the platform it lives on.

How we work

Three principles guide every product we ship.

01

Native, always

We build with Apple's own frameworks so apps feel like they belong on the device, not bolted on. Universal layouts, system fonts, and platform conventions throughout.

02

Privacy by default

Data stays on the user's device. No analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no behavioral tracking — and no accounts required to play.

03

Built to last

We commit to long-term support: regular updates, fast bug fixes, and meaningful day-one compatibility with new versions of iOS and iPadOS.
